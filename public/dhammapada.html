ABOUT THIS EDITION

Read The Dhammapada online at your own pace.

Choose a chapter or passage, turn pages at your own pace, or listen with the text-to-speech voice available on your device. Your latest reading position can be stored locally without creating an account.

What this reader includes

  • Page-by-page reading designed for desktop and mobile.
  • Direct navigation to the text’s available books, chapters, verses, ayahs, or passages.
  • Optional device text-to-speech with speed controls.
  • Reading progress saved in your browser when you choose to keep it on.
  • No advertising, paid subscription, or required account.

Edition note

The Dhammapada is one Buddhist text, not the whole Buddhist canon. Some verse numbers are combined in the selected source and remain combined in this reader.

Privacy and read-aloud

Reading choices are not stored in an application account. When “Remember my place” is enabled, the browser stores the current reading location locally. Read aloud uses speech services provided by the browser or operating system, so voice availability and pronunciation vary by device.
